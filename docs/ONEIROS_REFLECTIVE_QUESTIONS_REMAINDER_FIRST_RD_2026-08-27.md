## What the structure actually did

Three weak cases became remainder-asks:

- `child-falls-you-catch-air` — clearest win. RF: what remains in the hands after they caught only air and still stand as if holding something. B slots the child at the tap.
- `white-horse-empty-church` — RF asks the one hoof-strike then stillness. B asks the distance between dreamer and horse.
- `mouth-fills-with-seeds` — RF asks how the shoots stand without pot or water. B wraps `το ότι` plus speech-return.

`skin`, `funeral`, and `hug` stayed remainder-asks on both sides.

The other weak cases did not jump:

- `exam-blood-on-paper` — better opening (stain grows as you write around it), then “what happens on the page.”
- `stranger-matches-pace` — “what happened in that look,” not the matched pace that stopped.
- `elevator-coworker-silence` — “this small continuation of the route.”
- `closed-kiosk-at-noon` — same “what does the kiosk have such that you pass.”
- `gold-dust-from-ordinary-cup` — both invent a force. The leftover was dust after the cup went white.

Rich PASSes were not preserved. `house-becomes-boat` became an `ανάμεσα`. `chased-through-hospital` named the quiet call, then slotted the shaft; B made the withheld touch-or-fall the object.

## Why STOP

Success was not “safer.” Success was weak cases carrying the question the way skin / gold / house / hospital do, without losing those PASSes.

Remainder-first can produce that move (`child`, `horse`, `seeds`). It does not produce it reliably, and it can spend a remainder budget on a manufactured between (`house`) or a cause (`gold`). Pull is a tie (19 vs 18).

## Learning

Remainder-first did not fail as a recognition of remainder. The model can recognize a leftover and ask from it. **“Make the remainder the grammatical object” is not a sufficient control mechanism for selecting the right remainder.**

The write-side problem is largely closed. What remains is selection semantics: which dream event deserves to carry the question — what changed, failed to complete, or remained after the dream’s own movement — not another wording constraint.
