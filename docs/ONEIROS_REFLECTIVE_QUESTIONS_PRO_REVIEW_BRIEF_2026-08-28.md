# Oneiros Reflective Intelligence — Independent Professional Review Brief

Date: 2026-08-28

## Purpose

Evaluate whether the supplied reflective questions create a
genuinely alive, image-near post-Jungian encounter, while remaining faithful to
what the dreamer actually supplied. Mechanical schema success is not evidence of
human quality.

The review should answer four questions:

1. Which outputs would a person genuinely want to answer?
2. Which outputs deepen contact with the dream without explaining, diagnosing,
   leading, or inventing?
3. Which recurring failure families keep Oneiros safe but emotionally sterile?
4. Is the current proposed architecture a credible next candidate, or does it
   preserve the wrong abstraction?

## Review order

### Phase A — blind output review

Open only `BLIND_REVIEW/`.

For questions, score each output from `0–2` on:

- evidence fidelity;
- image specificity;
- psychological aliveness;
- psychic expansion;
- unforced ambiguity;
- human pull;
- genuine desire to answer;
- naturalness in the target language.

Then mark:

- `PASS`, `WEAK`, or `FAIL`;
- whether abstention would have served the dreamer better;
- the smallest phrase or structural choice that determined the verdict.

### Phase B — pattern diagnosis

Without opening the implementation notes yet, report:

1. the three strongest outputs and why they work;
2. the three weakest outputs and the exact failure mechanism;
3. repeated linguistic shells or emotional postures;
4. invented actions, motives, relationships, deficits, defenses, or waking-life
   premises;
5. questions that merely ask for a feeling without discovering a live psychic
   edge;
6. questions whose concrete verb or image genuinely carries the opening;
7. cases where no question would be the more mature Oneiros response;
8. whether each of the two abstentions is mature restraint or a missed live
   opening.

### Phase C — unblind and architecture review

Open `AFTER_BLIND_REVIEW/` only after Phase A and Phase B are recorded. Review the
version key, raw audit artifacts, R&D diagnosis, and proposed `v4.1.0`
director-to-`answer_target`-to-composer architecture.

Please assess:

- whether centrality selection improved across the sequence;
- whether the composer still defaults to portable “what was it like for you”
  forms even when attention is correctly directed;
- whether `answer_target` is a useful hard contract or another abstract layer;
- whether two calls are justified by the quality gain;
- whether the deterministic commit gate protects fidelity without becoming the
  hidden author;
- which `5–8` adversarial cases should form the next cost-controlled live gate.

## Hard-fail criteria

Any one of these is a hard failure:

- an invented waking-life premise;
- an unstaged relationship, motive, action, preference, or bodily event;
- treating absence or a non-event as evidence of defense, numbness, hidden lack,
  latent significance, integration, acceptance, harmlessness, or no injury;
- advice, prescription, diagnosis, or therapy-script language;
- a portable generic shell that could fit many unrelated dreams;
- reconstruction of an event the dream did not stage;
- asking the dreamer to predict, continue, or choose the dream scene as a
  substitute for reflection;
- more than one question movement;
- malformed or non-native target-language realization.
