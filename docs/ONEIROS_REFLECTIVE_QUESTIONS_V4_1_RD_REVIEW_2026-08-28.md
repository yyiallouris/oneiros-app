## Dialogue findings retained in v1.9.1

The later dialogue cycles moved in the right direction:

- explicit stopping takes priority over continued interpretation;
- brief yes/okay may be acknowledgment rather than material to decode;
- absence of pain does not prove numbness, defense, acceptance, integration, or absence of injury;
- grief responses name the person/feeling without giving the dream image invented grief or symbolic humanity;
- localized deterministic completion protects mature endings;
- chat remains zero-or-one question and often correctly abstains.

The last full dialogue packet produced `1` question and `15` abstentions with zero technical failures. That is a safety improvement, not automatic proof of depth. A later small, fluent trajectory review must confirm that appropriate openings are not being over-abstained.
