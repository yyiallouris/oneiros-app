These are screenshots of the running Expo web application in design-export mode, captured by Google Chrome at a fixed 390 × 844 viewport. They are not redrawn mockups.

## Rules standardized

1. Serif means dream title, short inward voice, or emotionally important silence. Sans means navigation, controls, metadata, settings, and system information.
2. Shared paper cards use one faint contour and restrained depth; no decorative inset border or top glow.
3. Insights empty-state marks use a quiet 88dp optical frame and preserve their original black-ink artwork.
4. Existing icon concepts remain intact. Navigation, Insights, and functional icons keep their distinct roles while sharing restrained ink character and optical balance.

## Intentionally unchanged

- Information architecture, flows, warm paper direction, and established palette.
- Feather, Insights sun, and all supplied Insights icon concepts/silhouettes.
- The approved drifting-page Journal icon, with no witness dot.
- Calendar and microphone raster artwork, and the existing functional action-icon paths.
- Perfect geometry where it is functional: pills, touch targets, inputs, and the floating navigation shelf.
- Long interpretation/report prose remains Inter with relaxed leading for sustained readability; serif is not used as generic atmosphere.
- Subscription tier cards retain their stronger semantic surfaces because their distinction communicates plan hierarchy rather than decoration.

## Full-app visual QA

The 36-screen contact sheet was reviewed through ten passes: hierarchy, icons, typography, spacing, geometry, Oneiros identity, generic-UI test, subtraction, emotional read, and final distance test. The final set was accepted only after reviewing complete screens together, not isolated components.
