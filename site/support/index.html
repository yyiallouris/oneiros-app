Include the email linked to your account, your device, and a short description of what happened if you are reporting a bug or access issue.

1. What to email us about

  • Email support for billing questions, restore problems, subscription access, and store-purchase issues.
  • Email support for privacy questions, export requests, restriction requests, account deletion, or data deletion.
  • Email support for sign-in trouble, locked-out access, or technical issues inside the app.

2. In-app routes

Signed-in users can also open Oneiros and use the Contact screen from the app menu. If you are locked out or having trouble signing in, use the login support option in the app or email us directly.

3. Data and deletion requests

For export, account deletion, or data deletion requests, please contact support@oneirosjournal.com from the email address connected to your Oneiros account when possible. This helps us verify the request safely.

4. Safety boundary

Oneiros support cannot provide therapy, diagnosis, medical care, mental health treatment, or crisis support. If you may harm yourself or someone else, feel in immediate danger, or need urgent help, contact local emergency services or a trusted crisis, medical, or mental health professional now.
