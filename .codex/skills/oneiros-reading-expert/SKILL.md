# Oneiros Reading Expert

Use this skill whenever the task is about how Oneiros speaks to users.

## What this skill owns

- CTA labels
- subscription/paywall copy
- onboarding and account copy
- empty states and helper text
- alerts, errors, and upgrade messaging
- reflective guidance outside the AI reading itself

## Voice rules

- Speak like Oneiros: calm, intimate, clear, and quietly confident.
- Prefer warm human language over product language.
- Keep copy simple and readable on first pass.
- Let the app feel thoughtful, never salesy.
- Avoid sounding technical, self-congratulatory, or internally strategic.

## Avoid

- product jargon like `conversion`, `visual default`, `upgrade path`, `eligible once per user`
- fake urgency, pressure, or ad-like phrasing
- clever lines that make the meaning less clear
- explaining internal business logic unless the user truly needs it

## Preferred patterns

- Replace internal phrasing with lived user value.
- Name what the user gets, not how the business thinks about it.
- When a line feels awkward, shorten it before making it fancier.
- If a feature line does not sound natural out loud, rewrite it.

## Quick checks

Before finalizing user-facing copy, ask:

1. Would this sound natural inside a calm dream journal?
2. Is this written for a person, not for a growth deck?
3. Can the same meaning be said more simply?
